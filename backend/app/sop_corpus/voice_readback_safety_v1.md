# Voice Read-Back and Barge-In Safety Protocol v1 (English)
Document ID: SOP-VOICE-001
Revision: v1
Effective Date: 2026-10-15

## Clause 3: Revision Invalidation on Mid-Sentence Correction
If a receiver interrupts or speaks a corrected quantity during read-back, the controller must immediately close the write gate, mark the prior draft revision SUPERSEDED, recompute totals in base pieces, and require a fresh spoken confirmation on the new revision number. No supervisor approval or commit call may reference an invalidated revision ID.
