## Clause 1: Ambiguous Pack Term Resolution
Vernacular terms such as peti, box, or pack vary by SKU between inner boxes and master cartons. Whenever a spoken unit is listed as ambiguous on the SKU master record, the agent must pause calculation, state the exact piece multiplier from the master record, and ask the dock receiver for explicit piece or master-carton confirmation before drafting a receipt revision.
