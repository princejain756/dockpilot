# GST e-Way Bill Verification Policy v3 (English)
Document ID: SOP-EWB-003
Revision: v3
Effective Date: 2026-09-15

## Clause 2: Expired or Mismatched e-Way Bill Hold
Before committing any Goods Receipt Note, the receiving gate must verify that the linked e-Way Bill status is ACTIVE and that its valid-until timestamp has not elapsed prior to vehicle arrival. If an e-Way Bill is marked EXPIRED or CANCELLED, automatic GRN posting must halt with status HOLD_EXCEPTION. The agent must surface the discrepancy flag for Supervisor or Finance review and must never render a binding legal or tax ruling on GST liability.
