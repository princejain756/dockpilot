## Clause 4: Partial Receipt Shortage and Supervisor Approval Threshold
When physical dock count is lower than Purchase Order expected pieces, the receiving system must calculate shortage strictly in base pieces (cartons multiplied by SKU pack size plus loose pieces). If total shortage value exceeds INR 25,000 on a single Goods Receipt Note, automated stock posting must be blocked. A separately authenticated Supervisor must review the spoken confirmation, verify the variance against the supplier invoice and e-Way Bill, and approve the exact draft revision before GRN commit. Upon commit, the system generates a DRAFT Supplier Debit Note Pack tied to the original invoice number without altering the original invoice record.
