# Damaged Carton Quarantine SOP v2 (English)
Document ID: SOP-QRN-002
Revision: v2
Effective Date: 2026-09-20

## Clause 2: Physical Segregation and Loose-Piece Recount
When cartons arrive crushed or wet, operators must open affected cartons, count intact loose pieces separately from sealed peti, and record only verified intact pieces in the Goods Receipt Note. Unaccepted or damaged items must remain unposted to available stock and linked to the supplier claim record.
